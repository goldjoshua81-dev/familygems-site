Privacy policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

The short version

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers, and it has no forms. We do not collect personal information on this site.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log technical information such as visitors' IP addresses for security and to keep the service running. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Purchases and messages

When you buy from us or message us, that happens on Etsy, and Etsy's privacy policy applies. We use the information Etsy shares with us, such as your name and shipping address, only to fulfill and support your order.

Links to other sites

Links to Etsy and other websites are governed by those sites' own policies.

Changes

If we add analytics, an email list or a contact form, we will update this page first.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Message us through our Etsy shop.
