Our crest

The emerald that started it all

When I was three or four, my dad bought a crisp apple-green Muzo emerald from Colombia, set in a 14k gold tension ring with gold nuggets down the sides. It hooked me for life. That emerald crowns our crest, and the diamond below it stands for the whole family.
