How we describe stones

No surprises

The same four promises on every stone page.

Seller-identified

Not guaranteed. Every stone is identified by us and described from our collection labels.

GIA-informed

Descriptions follow GIA-informed language. Lab reports are shown only when one exists.

Photographed as it is

Inclusions stay visible. Glow is added to the background only, never to the stone.

Unknown says “unknown”

No invented origins, treatments or value claims. Missing facts are marked plainly.
