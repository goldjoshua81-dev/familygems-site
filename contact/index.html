Contact us

The fastest way to reach us is Etsy Messages.

  1. Open our Etsy shop.
  2. Choose Message (or Contact shop owner).
  3. Tell us which stone you are asking about and what you need, such as measurements or more photos.

Questions about an existing order? Use the message thread on your Etsy order so all the details stay together.
